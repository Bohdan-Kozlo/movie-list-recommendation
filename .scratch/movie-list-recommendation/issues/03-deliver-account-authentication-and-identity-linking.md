# 03 — Deliver Account Authentication and Identity Linking

**What to build:** A visitor can register and sign in with email/password or Google OAuth, receive cookie-based sessions, and retain a single account when a verified Google email matches an existing email account.

**Blocked by:** 01 — Bootstrap the Reproducible Development Environment.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Email/password registration and login establish access and refresh sessions in cookies.
- [ ] Google OAuth creates or safely links an identity only for an exactly matching verified email.
